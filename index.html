About Us

The Fable Forest is a cozy, independently owned bookstore where stories come to life. Nestled in the heart of town, it offers a curated selection of new and classic titles across all genres, from literary fiction and fantasy to children's books and local authors. With warm lighting, comfortable reading nooks, and the scent of freshly brewed coffee from the adjoining café, The Fable Forest invites readers of all ages to explore, discover, and get lost in the magic of books. Whether you're searching for your next favorite novel or just want to browse, The Fable Forest is a welcoming space for book lovers and curious minds alike.

Featured Books

Each week, we handpick a selection of standout titles—new releases, hidden gems, and timeless favorites we think you'll love. Stop by The Fable Forest to see what’s on our shelf this week!

Life of Pi by Yann Martel

A modern classic, Life of Pi is a captivating tale of survival, faith, and imagination. When a young boy named Pi is stranded in the Pacific Ocean after a shipwreck, he must survive 227 dsays at sea—alone except for a Bengal tiger named Richard Parker. Both philosophical and adventurous, this novel explores the boundaries between reality and fiction, and the strength of the human spirit.

Braiding Sweetgrass by Robin Wall Kimmerer

A beautiful blend of science, storytelling, and Indigenous wisdom, Braiding Sweetgrass invites readers to see the natural world through a lens of reciprocity and respect. As a botanist and member of the Citizen Potawatomi Nation, Kimmerer weaves together ecological knowledge and personal narrative to explore our relationship with the Earth. Thoughtful, poetic, and deeply moving, this book is a call to remember that we are not separate from nature, but part of it.

The God of Small Things by Arundhati Roy

Winner of the Booker Prize, The God of Small Things is a richly woven story of family, forbidden love, and the lingering impact of political and social tensions in India. Set in Kerala, the novel follows fraternal twins Estha and Rahel as they uncover long-buried secrets from their childhood. With lyrical prose and a non-linear narrative, Arundhati Roy crafts a haunting and unforgettable exploration of how the smallest moments can shape entire lives.

Goodbye Tsugumi by Banana Yoshimoto

Delicate and introspective, Goodbye Tsugumi is a coming-of-age story set in a quiet seaside town in Japan. The novel follows Maria, a thoughtful young woman, and her frail but fiery cousin Tsugumi as they spend one last summer together before life pulls them in different directions. With understated beauty and emotional depth, Banana Yoshimoto explores themes of friendship, change, and the quiet power of memory.
